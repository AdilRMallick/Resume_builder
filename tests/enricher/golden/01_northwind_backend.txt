Software Engineer, Backend (New Grad) - Northwind Systems

About the role

Northwind Systems builds the settlement layer that moves money between regional banks and
the fintechs that sit on top of them. The Payments Core team owns the services that accept,
validate, and settle roughly four million transactions a day. We are hiring new graduates
onto the team for a summer 2026 start.

You will spend your first six months owning a real service. Recent new grads on the team
have shipped an idempotency layer for our transfer API, cut p99 latency on the ledger read
path by 60%, and rewritten the reconciliation job that closes our books every night.

What you will do

- Design, build, and operate backend services in Go and Python that process payment
  instructions end to end.
- Model and evolve schemas in PostgreSQL, and write the migrations that get them there
  without downtime.
- Participate in code review as both an author and a reviewer. Every change we ship is
  reviewed by someone else on the team.
- Take part in the team on-call rotation after your first three months, with a senior
  engineer shadowing you for the first two shifts.
- Write and maintain integration tests for the systems you own.

Basic qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related technical field, completed by
  August 2026.
- Proficiency in at least one statically typed language such as Go, Java, or C++.
- Solid grounding in data structures, algorithms, and complexity analysis.
- Working knowledge of SQL and relational database design.
- Experience with version control and collaborative development using Git.
- Strong written communication skills; we are a documentation-heavy team and most decisions
  are made in writing.

Preferred qualifications

- Internship or co-op experience building server-side software.
- Familiarity with Kubernetes or another container orchestration system.
- Exposure to distributed systems concepts: consensus, replication, idempotency, or
  exactly-once delivery.
- Experience with Apache Kafka or a comparable event streaming platform.
- Interest in payments, ledgers, or financial infrastructure.

Our stack

Go and Python services deployed to Kubernetes on AWS, PostgreSQL and Redis for state,
Kafka for the event bus, Terraform for infrastructure, and Datadog for observability. You
are not expected to know all of this on day one. You are expected to be curious about the
parts you have not seen.

Compensation and benefits

Base salary of $145,000 to $165,000, an annual bonus target of 10%, equity, unlimited PTO,
and full medical, dental, and vision coverage from your first day.
