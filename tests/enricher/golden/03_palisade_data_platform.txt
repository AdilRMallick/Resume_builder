Associate Software Engineer, Data Platform - Palisade Analytics

Palisade Analytics gives retail operators a single view of inventory across every warehouse,
store, and third-party marketplace they sell through. The Data Platform team owns the
pipelines that make that possible: roughly nine hundred daily batch jobs and a streaming
path that carries about two billion events a month.

This posting is for our 2026 new graduate cohort. You will be paired with a mentor for your
first two quarters and rotate through two of the three sub-teams before choosing where to
land.

In this role you will

- Build and maintain batch data pipelines using Apache Airflow, and own the dags you write
  through to production.
- Write transformation logic in Python and SQL against a Snowflake warehouse.
- Contribute to our streaming ingestion path, which is built on Kafka and Apache Flink.
- Instrument pipelines with data quality checks so that a bad upstream feed fails loudly
  instead of quietly corrupting a dashboard.
- Debug production incidents with the team, and write the postmortem afterwards.

Minimum qualifications

- Currently pursuing or recently completed a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer
  Science, Data Science, Statistics, or a related field.
- Strong programming ability in Python.
- Demonstrated proficiency in SQL, including joins, window functions, and query optimization.
- Coursework or project experience in databases or distributed systems.
- Ability to communicate technical tradeoffs clearly to non-technical stakeholders.
- Graduation between December 2025 and June 2026.

Preferred qualifications

- Hands-on experience with a cloud data warehouse such as Snowflake, BigQuery, or Redshift.
- Familiarity with Apache Spark or a comparable distributed processing framework.
- Experience with dbt or a similar analytics engineering tool.
- Exposure to infrastructure as code, particularly Terraform.
- Understanding of dimensional modeling and slowly changing dimensions.
- Contributions to open source data tooling.

Working here

The Data Platform team is fully remote within the United States, with an optional office in
Denver and two on-site team weeks a year. We run a four-day release cycle, no deploys on
Fridays, and a genuine on-call rotation that pays for the interruption.

Palisade Analytics is committed to building a team that reflects the operators we serve. We
strongly encourage applications from candidates whose backgrounds are underrepresented in
data engineering.
