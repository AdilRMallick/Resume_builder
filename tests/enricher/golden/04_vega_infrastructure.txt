New Grad Software Engineer, Infrastructure - Vega Robotics

Vega Robotics builds autonomous material handling robots for warehouses. There are about
eleven hundred of our units on customer floors today, and every one of them talks to the
fleet services our Infrastructure team runs. When our platform has a bad minute, someone's
distribution center stops moving.

What the team does

The Infrastructure team owns the compute, networking, and deployment tooling that the rest
of engineering builds on: our Kubernetes clusters, the CI system, the observability stack,
and the over-the-air update path that ships new firmware to a robot in the field.

Your first year

- Own a piece of our internal developer platform, most likely the build and deploy path that
  every other team uses many times a day.
- Write infrastructure as code in Terraform, and the Go tooling that wraps it.
- Improve our observability: we run Prometheus and Grafana, and there is real work to do in
  making alerts mean something.
- Automate what is currently manual. We keep a running list of toil and we take it seriously.
- Join the on-call rotation for platform services in your second quarter.

Requirements

- BS or MS in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or equivalent experience, graduating
  by summer 2026.
- Programming experience in Go, Python, or Rust.
- A working mental model of Linux: processes, file descriptors, permissions, and networking
  basics.
- Understanding of how containers work, not just how to run docker build.
- Familiarity with Git-based workflows and continuous integration.
- Willingness to be on-call and a level head when something is broken.

Bonus points

- Experience operating Kubernetes, whether at work, at school, or in a home lab.
- Any exposure to Prometheus, Grafana, or another metrics and alerting stack.
- Scripting in Bash and comfort living in a terminal.
- Experience with an embedded or real-time system, or any exposure to ROS.
- Contributions to an open source infrastructure project.
- Interest in robotics. You do not need robotics experience; you do need to find it
  interesting, because you will hear about it every day.

Logistics

The role is on-site four days a week in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. We provide relocation
assistance. Vega Robotics sponsors H-1B and other employment visas for this position.

We are an equal opportunity employer and value diversity at our company.
