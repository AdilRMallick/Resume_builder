Software Engineer, Machine Learning (University Grad) - Ledgerline

Ledgerline detects fraud for mid-market lenders. Our models score about forty thousand loan
applications a day, and a false negative costs a customer real money, so we care a great
deal about being right and about being able to explain why we were right.

The Applied ML team turns models into production services. We are not a research lab. The
distance between an idea in a notebook and a decision in production is about two weeks here,
and shortening that distance is most of the job.

What you will work on

- Train, evaluate, and ship supervised models for fraud and credit risk using Python,
  PyTorch, and scikit-learn.
- Build the feature pipelines those models depend on, in Spark and SQL.
- Deploy models as services, monitor them for drift, and own the retraining schedule.
- Write the offline evaluation that decides whether a model ships, including fairness and
  disparate impact analysis.
- Explain a model's behavior to a risk officer who has never written a line of code.

Minimum qualifications

- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics, or a related
  quantitative field, graduating between December 2025 and August 2026.
- Strong programming skills in Python.
- Coursework or project experience in machine learning, including a working understanding of
  overfitting, regularization, and evaluation metrics beyond accuracy.
- Familiarity with pandas and NumPy.
- Proficiency in SQL for exploratory data analysis.
- Ability to write clearly. Every model we ship is accompanied by a written evaluation.

Preferred qualifications

- Experience with PyTorch or TensorFlow on a project of nontrivial size.
- Exposure to gradient boosted trees, particularly XGBoost or LightGBM, which remain the
  workhorses of tabular fraud detection.
- Familiarity with Apache Spark for large-scale feature computation.
- Experience with MLflow or another experiment tracking system.
- Some exposure to Docker and to deploying a model behind an API.
- Interest in fraud, risk, or adversarial settings where the data distribution fights back.

Interview process

A recruiter call, a Python and SQL screen, and a virtual onsite with four rounds: applied ML
depth, a coding round, an ML system design round, and a conversation about a project you
have done. We share the rubric with you beforehand because we do not think secrecy makes the
signal better.

Ledgerline participates in E-Verify. This role is based in Chicago, Illinois with a hybrid
schedule of three days in the office.
