Software Engineer I, Full Stack - Cobalt Health

Cobalt Health is a clinical documentation company. Our product sits between a physician and
their electronic health record and removes the two hours of charting that currently happen
after every clinic day. We are a fifty-person company with eleven engineers, and we ship to
production several times a week.

The team

You would join the Provider Experience team, which owns everything a clinician actually
touches: the web application, the mobile companion, and the API that backs both. The team is
four engineers, one designer, and a product manager who used to be a nurse.

Responsibilities

- Build user-facing features in React and TypeScript, from the first Figma review through to
  the production rollout.
- Implement and extend the REST APIs those features depend on. Our backend is Python with
  FastAPI.
- Write unit and end-to-end tests. We use pytest on the backend and Playwright for the
  browser flows.
- Work directly with clinicians during design reviews and usability sessions.
- Help keep our CI/CD pipeline healthy. Everyone on the team is expected to be able to
  unblock a broken build.

What we are looking for

- A degree in Computer Science, Human-Computer Interaction, or equivalent practical
  experience.
- Fluency in JavaScript or TypeScript and comfort with modern React, including hooks and
  component composition.
- Some experience with a backend language; Python is what we use, but we care much more
  about whether you can reason about an HTTP request than about which language taught you.
- Familiarity with HTML and CSS, and an eye for when an interface feels wrong.
- Comfort reading code you did not write. Most of your first month will be reading.
- Excellent communication skills and the patience to work with users who are not engineers.

Nice to have

- Experience with Docker and containerized local development.
- Exposure to healthcare data standards such as HL7 or FHIR.
- Prior work with PostgreSQL, including writing queries you would be willing to run in
  production.
- Any experience with accessibility standards and WCAG compliance.
- A portfolio, side project, or open source contribution you can walk us through.

How we hire

One recruiter conversation, one technical screen in the language of your choice, and an
onsite consisting of a systems discussion, a pairing session on our real codebase, and a
conversation with the product manager. We give a decision within three business days of the
onsite.

Cobalt Health does not sponsor employment visas at this time for this role. Applicants must
be authorized to work in the United States.
